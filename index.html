Cumulative savings from not owning a gas car, year by year over 5 years, against the price of a new Tesla.

This adds up what you'd otherwise spend on maintenance, repairs, and gas each year if you keep the gas car, and tracks it against a Tesla's price. It doesn't include the Tesla's own purchase cost or financing, insurance, or depreciation on either vehicle.
